Damaged or Faulty on Arrival?

If your item arrives damaged, faulty, or not working, contact us within 5 days of delivery. To process a replacement or refund, we need:

  1. A photo of the product
  2. A photo or video clearly showing the damage/defect
  3. A photo of the shipping label on the outer packaging

Please keep the original packaging until your claim is resolved. Claims submitted after 5 days, or without the required photos, cannot be fully honored. Once we receive your evidence, we'll arrange a replacement or refund — no need to ship the item back.
